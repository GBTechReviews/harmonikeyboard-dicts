Tatoeba sentence data - licence and attribution
================================================

The next-word context packs under packs/ (en_ngrams.*, pl_ngrams.*, de_ngrams.*,
es_ngrams.*, fr_ngrams.*) - English, Polish, German, Spanish and French - are
DERIVED from sentence data published by the Tatoeba Project.

Source:        Tatoeba Project - https://tatoeba.org
